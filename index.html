I'm an AI engineer and computer vision researcher. I received my PhD in Computer Science from York University in 2024, advised by Prof. Michael S. Brown in the Computational Vision and Imaging Lab. Before that, I earned an MSc from POSTECH in South Korea.

My doctoral research focused on color science for camera imaging: recovering the wide-gamut colors that are lost when photos are converted from ProPhoto RGB to sRGB. I developed three approaches (a deep network, metadata-based color subsampling, and a lightweight MLP), released large-scale datasets, and published at CVPR and CIC. The GamutMLP work with Adobe led to a granted US patent. During my PhD I also interned at Samsung AI Center Toronto, on deep-learning white balance for smartphone cameras, and at Borealis AI, on deep survival analysis.

Today I'm an AI Engineer at Sunvair, building LLM-agent platforms that connect language models to enterprise data and tools. I continue to collaborate on research in vision-language models and video retrieval.
